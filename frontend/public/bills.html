Bills & Services

Everyday payments from the same account you save with.

Services

  • Airtime for MTN, Airtel, Glo and 9mobile numbers.
  • Data bundles from each network's current price list.
  • Electricity: prepaid tokens and postpaid bills. The meter is checked with the distribution company before you pay.
  • Cable TV: renew or change your package after the smartcard is checked.
  • Exam PINs such as result-checker PINs.

Which services and providers are available can change; the app shows what can be bought right now.

How a purchase works

  1. Enter the details. Prices come from the provider and any fee is shown before you confirm.
  2. Approve with your transaction PIN and an emailed code, or with biometrics in the Android app.
  3. Pay from your ACHIEVER Wallet or with Paystack.
  4. The purchase is sent to a licensed bill provider. It is marked successful only when the provider confirms delivery; if it cannot be delivered you are refunded.
  5. Tokens and PINs appear in your transaction details, shown only to you.
