Save with a collector

Individual savings with a collector you trust, and a record you can check at any time.

Important: This page explains how ACHIEVER works in plain language. It is not legal advice and has not yet been reviewed by Nigerian legal and compliance professionals; it must be reviewed before public launch. ACHIEVER is not a bank, is not licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria as a deposit-taking institution, and money recorded in ACHIEVER is not covered by NDIC deposit insurance.

How collector savings work

  • A collector is approved by ACHIEVER first: they verify their identity and accept a written undertaking.
  • You join a collector's plan by invitation and see the term and the collector's commission before you start.
  • You save flexible amounts during the term. Each deposit is confirmed with the payment provider before it is recorded.
  • At the end of the term your savings, less the agreed commission, are due back to you.

Different from Osusu

Collector savings are individual: your money is not pooled and paid to other people in turn. ACHIEVER keeps the two products separate, with separate rules.
