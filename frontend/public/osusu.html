Osusu groups, kept on record

Rotational group savings, with every contribution and payout on one shared record.

Important: This page explains how ACHIEVER works in plain language. It is not legal advice and has not yet been reviewed by Nigerian legal and compliance professionals; it must be reviewed before public launch. ACHIEVER is not a bank, is not licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria as a deposit-taking institution, and money recorded in ACHIEVER is not covered by NDIC deposit insurance.

How an Osusu group works on ACHIEVER

  • The organiser sets the contribution amount, the schedule (for example weekly or monthly), the number of members and the payout order before the group starts.
  • Members read and accept the group's rules before joining. Once the group starts, its financial terms are locked.
  • Every cycle, each member pays the same contribution. A payment counts only after Paystack confirms it to ACHIEVER.
  • When a cycle is fully paid, the pool goes to the member whose turn it is, in the agreed order.
  • Members who have already been paid keep contributing until everyone has had their turn.

Why groups use ACHIEVER

  • One record for everyone: who has paid, who is due and who has been paid out.
  • Accountable organisers: organisers verify their identity and accept a written undertaking.
  • Reminders: members are reminded before contributions are due, and late payments are flagged.
  • Talk in one place: group chat, voice and video calls, and scheduled meetings.
  • Disputes: problems are raised in the app and reviewed against the records.
